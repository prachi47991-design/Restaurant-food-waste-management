Welcome to Food Waste Management

Food waste is a common problem in restaurants. Food may be wasted because of overproduction, spoilage, incorrect preparation and unsold items.

This platform helps restaurants record their food waste, understand the reasons behind it and identify recurring sources of waste.

What This Platform Helps With

  • Record food waste
  • Organize waste by food category
  • Identify reasons for waste
  • Track quantity and date
  • Monitor different departments
  • Identify recurring waste patterns

Problem Statement

Restaurants discard food due to overproduction, spoilage, incorrect preparation and unsold items. Managers often lack visibility into where waste is occurring.

Common Causes of Food Waste

  1. Overproduction
  2. Food Spoilage
  3. Incorrect Preparation
  4. Unsold Food

Project Objective

The objective of this platform is to develop a system that records food waste by category and identifies recurring sources of waste.

Main Goals

  • Record food waste systematically
  • Understand major waste sources
  • Track waste quantity
  • Identify repeated waste patterns
  • Support better waste management decisions
